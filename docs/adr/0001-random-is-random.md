# Random is random: the record is the only way back

The tool offered replay: the seed on the sheet re-ran the same character,
which also carried interactive resumption, a provenance footer, a seed on
every record, a load-bearing dice consumption order, and goldens whose
fixtures were seeds found by search. Nothing in the referee reports asked for
replay, and each of those parts had needed its own rule or repair. Decided
2026-11-07 (#200): replay is withdrawn, the record carries every die and
every choice and is the only way back to a character, and fixtures state
their dice rather than a seed that reaches them. The cost is that a stopped
interactive session is lost and a referee who keeps only the sheet cannot
recover the character. A `replay` that re-walks a record stays possible and
is deliberately not built until a referee asks.

Withdraws from `docs/PRD.md`: Determinism.

Amended by ADR 0006: the record that is the only way back is the note's
generation record, and a `replay` would need a record a tool can read.
